Who We Are

The Windsor Coding Club is a student-run community at the University of Windsor dedicated to growing the next generation of software engineers, designers, and innovators. Whether you are writing your first line of Python or shipping production-grade web apps, you will find a place here.

Every week we host workshops, hackathons, and casual coding sessions where members learn from each other, build real projects, and prepare for tech careers.

Why Join Us?

  • Weekly hands-on workshops on web, AI, mobile, and systems programming
  • Mentorship from senior students and industry professionals
  • Access to exclusive hackathons with cash prizes and swag
  • Networking events with recruiters from top tech companies
  • A friendly community that makes learning to code fun
